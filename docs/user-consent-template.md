# BenefitLens User Consent Template

**Target Audience:** Low-income unorganised-sector workers.
**Language:** To be translated and read aloud via TTS in Telugu/Hindi.

## Consent Text (English Reference)

"Welcome to BenefitLens. We are here to help you find government schemes and understand financial documents. 

To give you the right advice, we need to know some basic details about your age, job, and income. 
We **do not** ask for your Aadhaar, PAN, or Bank Account numbers. 
When you speak to us, your voice is used only to understand your question and is not saved. 
When you show us a document, we read it to explain it to you, and then we forget it.

We will never share your details with anyone, and we will never apply for anything on your behalf without you doing it yourself.

Do you agree to share your basic details with us so we can help you?"

## DPDP Act Alignment
- **Notice**: Explains the exact purpose (finding schemes, explaining documents).
- **Data Types**: Clearly states what is collected (age, job, income) and what is explicitly avoided (Aadhaar, PAN).
- **Ephemeral Processing**: Clarifies that voice and documents are processed and dropped, not retained.
- **Affirmative Action**: Requires an active "Yes" (spoken or tapped) before the profile is created.
